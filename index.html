01 / THE SYSTEM

The GTM BDR
is a market sensor.

Not a call-volume machine. Each BDR owns a named account set, maps the buying group, runs a coordinated path across channels, and returns structured market intelligence to the team.

The operating rule: one account, one owner, one next best action.

01

Prioritize with evidence

Rank accounts by fit, mobility pain, buying signals, and reachable stakeholders—not list size alone.

OUTPUT → TIERED ACCOUNT BOOK
02

Personalize the problem

Lead with the operating cost of unreliable workforce movement, tailored to the sector and role.

OUTPUT → RELEVANT POINT OF VIEW
03

Orchestrate every touch

Combine automated reminders with human judgment. Each channel advances the same account hypothesis.

OUTPUT → COORDINATED CADENCE
04

Turn activity into learning

Capture objections, timing, incumbent setup, demand shape, and next steps as structured CRM data.

OUTPUT → COMPOUNDING SIGNALS

04 / THE OUTREACH PATH

12 touches. 15 business days.
One connected narrative.

Change the channel, not the story. Every step earns the right to the next one.

DAY 01EMAIL

Problem hypothesis

Role + sector relevance, one operational pain, one credible outcome, low-friction question.

DAY 01LINKEDIN

Research + follow

View, follow, and engage with context. Send a clean connection request—no pitch wall.

DAY 02CALL

Validate the hypothesis

Ask how employee mobility is handled today; capture the operating model and pain.

DAY 04EMAIL

Use-case proof

Share the most relevant SWVL capability: routing, managed operations, or SaaS visibility.

DAY 05LINKEDIN

Insight touch

Reference a real business trigger or offer a short benchmark—not a generic nudge.

DAY 06CALL

Second call

Use a new angle: productivity, cost, safety, control, or workforce access.

DAY 08WHATSAPP

Permission-based note

Only with valid consent or an established business context. Short, human, and easy to decline.

DAY 10EMAIL

Mini business case

Frame current-state cost, desired outcome, and what a 20-minute discovery would test.

DAY 11CALL

Multi-thread

Reach the second relevant role and connect the operational and executive narratives.

DAY 12LINKEDIN

Voice note / message

Use only when appropriate; summarize the hypothesis in under 30 seconds.

DAY 14EMAIL

Decision email

Offer three clear paths: explore now, revisit later, or redirect to the right owner.

DAY 15CALL

Close the loop

Confirm disposition, set a nurture date, and preserve the learning in HubSpot.

Channel guardrail: WhatsApp and personal-phone outreach should follow local law, company policy, documented consent/legitimate basis, and clear opt-out handling. Do not treat every enriched number as permission to message.
