THE MULTI-CHANNEL IDEA

Maximum market coverage.
Coordinated communication.

The same lead may be reachable on several channels. The BDR does not wait for one channel to fail before using another. Each channel creates a different opportunity to be seen, recognized, and answered—while HubSpot keeps the activity connected.

04Calls create live qualification
